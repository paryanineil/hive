Ignition Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Ignition is the task and project management app operated by V12 Infotech at erp2.v12infotech.com/ignition, for V12 Infotech's team members. This policy explains what data Ignition handles and why, including data from Google Calendar when you choose to connect it.

Data Ignition stores

Ignition stores the projects, tasks, checklists, notes, comments and attachments that you and your team create, along with your account details (name and email address) needed to sign you in and assign work. This data is kept on V12 Infotech's own server.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Ignition asks Google for permission to manage your calendars (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar scope), and uses it only to keep your tasks and your calendar in sync:

  • It creates a calendar for Ignition tasks in your Google account, and adds, updates and removes events in it for tasks that have dates.
  • It reads changes you make to those events, such as a new date, time or title, so the matching task in Ignition can be updated.

Ignition does not read, change or delete your other calendars or events. The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored encrypted on V12 Infotech's server.

Ignition's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing

Your data is visible to the members of your team in Ignition according to the project's sharing settings. V12 Infotech does not sell your data or share it with third parties, except where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

Task data is kept for as long as V12 Infotech uses Ignition. You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time; Ignition then stops syncing and its stored Google tokens are removed. You can also revoke Ignition's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for your data to be deleted, contact us at the address below.
